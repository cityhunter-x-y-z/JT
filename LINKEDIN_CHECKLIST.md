### 2. Headline (10 minutes) - MOST IMPORTANT!

**Current format:** "Product Designer at [Company]" ← BAD (wastes space)

**New format:** Keywords + Location + Specialization

**Formula:**
```
Product Designer | [Top Skill 1] + [Top Skill 2] | [Specialty/Industry] | Bangalore
```

**Examples based on your experience:**

If you specialize in **Mobile Design:**
```
Product Designer | Mobile UX | User Research & Prototyping | 3+ Years | Bangalore
```

If you specialize in **Fintech:**
```
Product Designer | Fintech UX | Payment Flows & Mobile Design | Bangalore
```

If you focus on **B2B/SaaS:**
```
Senior Product Designer | B2B SaaS | Design Systems & Dashboard Design | Bangalore
```

If you're **Early career:**
```
Product Designer | Mobile-First UX | Prototyping & User Testing | 2 Years | Bangalore
```

- [ ] **Pick the one with most relevant keywords**
- [ ] **Update LinkedIn headline NOW**

## ✅ PHASE 3: Optimization Settings (10 minutes)

### 8. Open to Work Settings

- [ ] **Turn on "Open to Work" - RECRUITERS ONLY**
  - Click "Open to" button (under headline)
  - Select "Finding a new job"
  - Choose: **"All LinkedIn members"** OR **"Recruiters only"**
    - Choose "Recruiters only" if currently employed (safer)
  - Job titles: "Product Designer", "UX Designer", "Senior Product Designer"
  - Locations: Add "Bangalore", "Karnataka, India"
  - Start date: "Immediately" or "Flexible"
  - Click "Add to profile" or "All LinkedIn members"

**⚠️ Important:** "Recruiters only" = safe. "All LinkedIn members" = your current employer might see!

---

### 9. Public Profile Settings

- [ ] **Make profile public (searchable on Google):**
  - Settings & Privacy → Visibility → "Public profile"
  - Toggle ON
  - Customize URL: linkedin.com/in/yourname-productdesigner
  - Check: "Make profile visible to everyone" is ON

- [ ] **Profile viewing options:**
  - Settings & Privacy → Visibility → "Profile viewing options"
  - Select: "Your name and headline"
  - (Recruiters want to see who viewed their profile)

---

### 10. Job Search Preferences

- [ ] **Set up job alerts:**
  - LinkedIn Jobs → Search "Product Designer Bangalore"
  - Click "Create alert" → Daily emails
  - Set filters: Experience level, job type (Full-time)

## ✅ PHASE 4: Activity & Engagement (Ongoing - 15 mins/day)

### 11. Weekly Posting Strategy (1-2 posts per week)

**What to post:**

**Monday/Tuesday:**
- Design insights: "3 things I learned redesigning [feature]"
- Portfolio updates: Share a case study
- Design process: Behind-the-scenes photo of whiteboarding

**Wednesday/Thursday:**
- Design tips: "One small change that increased conversion by X%"
- Tool tips: "How I use Figma's [feature] to speed up work"
- Industry commentary: Thoughts on new design trends

**Example post template:**
```
Most designers make this mistake when designing [common feature]...

[Hook - surprising statement or question]

Here's what I learned from [experience]:
• [Learning 1]
• [Learning 2]
• [Learning 3]

[Takeaway or question for audience]

What's your approach? Drop a comment 👇

#ProductDesign #UXDesign #Bangalore
```

**Your Action:**
- [ ] Write 2 posts this week
- [ ] Schedule: Tuesday and Friday
- [ ] Post between 9 AM - 5 PM IST
- [ ] Add 1 image/screenshot
- [ ] Use 2-3 hashtags: #ProductDesign #UXDesign #Bangalore

### 12. Daily Engagement (15 minutes)

**Morning routine (10 mins):**
- [ ] Scroll LinkedIn feed
- [ ] Comment on 3-5 posts (meaningful comments, not just "Great!")
- [ ] Like 10-15 relevant posts

**Who to engage with:**
- Recruiters posting about design roles
- Design leaders at companies you target
- Fellow designers sharing work
- Company pages of target companies

**Comment template:**
```
[Your perspective based on experience] + [Question or additional insight]
```

**Example:**
Instead of: "Great insights!"

Write: "This resonates - we found similar patterns in our checkout redesign. Did you test this across different user segments, or was the behavior uniform?"
