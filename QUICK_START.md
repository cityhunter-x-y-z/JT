# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Job Search System

## Getting Your First Interview in 30 Days

This guide will help you go from "getting auto-rejected" to "landing interviews" by focusing on quality, personalization, and direct outreach.

---

## Week 1: Foundation (Setup Week)

### Day 1-2: Profile Optimization (4 hours total)
- [ ] **LinkedIn Profile** - [Follow this guide](docs/linkedin_optimization.md)
  - Update headline with keywords
  - Rewrite about section with metrics
  - Add 3 items to Featured section
  - Update top 3 skills
  - Turn on "Open to work" (recruiters only)

- [ ] **Portfolio Review** - [Use this checklist](docs/portfolio_checklist.md)
  - Do you have 2-3 case studies?
  - Do they show process, not just final designs?
  - Do they include metrics/impact?
  - Is your portfolio mobile-friendly and fast?

### Day 3: System Setup (2 hours)
- [ ] **Create Google Sheet** - [Template structure](sheets_template/template_structure.csv)
  - Create sheet named "Job Hunt Tracker"
  - Add tabs: Job Tracker, Companies Database, Hiring Managers, Email Templates, Weekly Metrics
  - Populate with column headers

- [ ] **Set up Google Colab** - [Email generator](notebooks/01_email_generator.ipynb)
  - Open notebook in Google Colab
  - Connect to your Google Sheet
  - Fill in YOUR_INFO section
  - Test with one job

### Day 4-5: Target Company List (3 hours)
- [ ] **Research 50 Target Companies in Bangalore**

  **Where to find them:**
  - LinkedIn Jobs: Search "Product Designer Bangalore"
  - Naukri.com: Filter by Bangalore + Product Design
  - AngelList/Wellfound: Startups hiring designers
  - Company websites: Careers pages

  **Companies to consider:**
  - Fintech: Razorpay, PhonePe, CRED, Zerodha, Paytm
  - E-commerce: Flipkart, Meesho, Nykaa, Myntra
  - Food/Delivery: Swiggy, Zomato, Dunzo
  - B2B SaaS: Freshworks, Zoho, Chargebee
  - Global: Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Adobe (Bangalore offices)
  - Startups: Check AngelList for recently funded companies

  **Add to your sheet:**
  - Company name
  - Why you're interested (specific reason!)
  - LinkedIn company page
  - Current openings (if any)
  - Priority (A/B/C)

## Week 2: First Applications (Quality over Quantity)

**Goal: Send 5-8 QUALITY applications + direct emails**

### Daily Routine (1.5-2 hours/day)

#### Morning (45 mins): Research
1. Pick 2 companies from your target list
2. For each company, research:
   - What do they build? (Products/features)
   - Recent news? (Funding, launches, blog posts)
   - Design team? (Check LinkedIn)
   - Design philosophy? (Check company blog)
3. Add detailed notes to your tracker

#### Afternoon (1 hour): Apply
1. **Find the job posting** (if not posted, cold outreach)
2. **Find hiring manager:**
   - LinkedIn search: "[Company] design manager"
   - Company website: Team page
   - LinkedIn: Company page → People → Filter by Design
3. **Get email address:**
   - Hunter.io (free: 25/month)
   - Company website contact page
   - Email pattern: firstname@company.com
4. **Customize your resume:**
   - Add keywords from job description
   - Highlight relevant projects
   - Match your experience to their needs
5. **Generate personalized email** - Use Colab notebook
   - Fill in research_notes with SPECIFIC details
   - Generate email
   - REVIEW and add personal touches
   - Send from Gmail (don't auto-send!)
6. **Apply on company website** (if they have posting)
7. **Update tracker:**
   - Mark as "Applied"
   - Add date
   - Set follow-up date (7 days later)

#### Evening (15 mins): LinkedIn Activity
- Comment on 2-3 posts in your feed
- Connect with 1-2 people (designers, recruiters)
- Like relevant posts

### Week 2 Goals:
- [ ] Research 15 companies (3/day × 5 days)
- [ ] Send 8 applications with direct emails
- [ ] LinkedIn: Connect with 10 people
- [ ] LinkedIn: Comment on 10 posts
- [ ] Optional: Post 1 design insight

## Week 3: Follow-ups + More Applications

**Goal: Follow up on Week 2 applications + Send 8 more applications**

### Follow-up Strategy

**For applications sent 5-7 days ago with no response:**

1. Open your tracker → Filter by "Applied" + follow-up date
2. For each application:
   - Use [follow-up email template](email_templates/follow_up.txt)
   - Keep it SHORT (under 75 words)
   - Add NEW information if possible (new case study, relevant insight)
   - Send from Gmail
   - Update tracker: Mark as "Followed Up"
   - Set 2nd follow-up date (7 days later)

### Continue Applying
- Research 3 companies daily
- Send 8 more quality applications
- Direct email to hiring managers

### LinkedIn Activity
- Post 1 design insight or case study highlight
- Comment on posts
- Engage with responses to your post

### Week 3 Goals:
- [ ] Follow up on 5-8 applications from Week 2
- [ ] Send 8 new applications
- [ ] LinkedIn: Post 1 insight
- [ ] LinkedIn: Engage with 15 people

## Week 4: Optimize + Scale

**Goal: Review what's working, double down**

### Friday Review Session (1 hour)

1. **Open your tracker** - Calculate metrics:
   - Applications sent: __
   - Direct emails sent: __
   - Responses received: __
   - Response rate: __ % (responses ÷ applications × 100)
   - Phone screens: __
   - Interviews: __

2. **Analyze:**
   - **If response rate < 5%:**
     - ⚠️ Not personalizing enough
     - Review resume and portfolio
     - Get feedback from designer friends
     - Are you mentioning specific products/features?

   - **If response rate 5-10%:**
     - ✅ On track, keep going
     - Try more direct outreach
     - Experiment with email templates

   - **If response rate > 10%:**
     - 🎉 Great! Your approach works
     - Consider scaling up volume slightly
     - Focus on converting responses to interviews

3. **Adjust strategy for Week 4:**
   - What's working? Do more of it.
   - What's not working? Fix or stop doing it.
   - Which sources get best response? Focus there.

### Week 4 Goals:
- [ ] Follow up on Week 3 applications
- [ ] Send 8-10 applications (scale up if response rate good)
- [ ] Review metrics and adjust
- [ ] Update portfolio if needed
- [ ] Prepare for interviews (practice!)

- Research + apply to 2-3 companies daily

**Friday (1 hour):**

- Review metrics

- Plan next week

### Monthly Review

- Resume: Refresh with recent work
- LinkedIn: Post 2-4 times per month
- Metrics: What's your trend? Improving or stagnating?

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Week 1-2 Mistakes:
- ❌ Skipping profile optimization (your LinkedIn is working 24/7!)
- ❌ Not setting up tracking system (you'll lose track fast)
- ❌ Applying before researching (leads to generic applications)

### Week 3-4 Mistakes:
- ❌ Giving up too early (it takes 2-3 weeks to see results)
- ❌ Not following up (50% of interviews come from follow-ups)
- ❌ Applying to 100+ jobs (quantity ≠ quality)

### Ongoing Mistakes:
- ❌ Using Easy Apply exclusively (2-3% response rate)
- ❌ Not personalizing emails (obvious template = ignored)
- ❌ Forgetting to update tracker (can't improve without data)
- ❌ Not engaging on LinkedIn (reduces your visibility)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "I'm not getting ANY responses"
**Possible issues:**
1. Resume/Portfolio needs work → Get feedback from 3 designers
2. Not personalizing enough → Show your emails to someone, are they generic?
3. Applying to wrong roles → Are you qualified? Too junior/senior?
4. LinkedIn profile weak → Recruiters checking you out and passing?

**Action:**
- Pause applications for 2-3 days
- Fix root cause (usually #1 or #2)
- Test with 3 applications
- If still no response, get professional resume review

### "I'm getting responses but no interviews"
**Possible issues:**
1. Phone screen skills need work
2. Portfolio doesn't back up resume
3. Not asking good questions

**Action:**
- Practice phone screens with friends
- Record yourself (awkward but effective)
- Research company better before calls
- Prepare 3-4 questions about role/team

### "I'm getting interviews but no offers"
**Possible issues:**
1. Interview skills need practice
2. Portfolio case studies lack depth
3. Not showing culture fit
4. Technical/design skills gap

**Action:**
- Practice with mock interviews (ADPList.org)
- Improve portfolio case studies (show more process)
- Research company culture, values
- Identify skill gaps, fill them
