## 🎯 How to Research Each Company (15-minute Checklist)

For each company you're interested in:

### 1. Company Research (5 mins)
- [ ] Visit their website - what do they build?
- [ ] Check LinkedIn company page - how many employees?
- [ ] Recent news? (Google "[Company] funding" or "[Company] news")
- [ ] Glassdoor rating? (glassdoor.co.in)

### 2. Design Team Research (5 mins)
- [ ] LinkedIn: Search "[Company] design" - find designers
- [ ] Who's the design lead/manager?
- [ ] Do they have a design blog? (Check Medium, company blog)
- [ ] Check Dribbble/Behance for company work

### 3. Product Research (5 mins)
- [ ] Download their app / visit their website
- [ ] Use the product for 10 minutes
- [ ] What's impressive? What could be better?
- [ ] Check App Store reviews - what do users say?

### 4. Add to Your Sheet
- [ ] Company name
- [ ] Why you're interested (SPECIFIC reason!)
- [ ] LinkedIn company URL
- [ ] Design team lead (if found)
- [ ] Priority (A/B/C)
- [ ] Notes (1-2 sentences)

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Tier 2/3 India Focus:**
Many Bangalore companies (PhonePe, Meesho, Paytm, BharatPe) focus on Tier 2/3 Indian users. If you have experience designing for:
- Low connectivity
- Regional languages
- Simplified interfaces
- First-time internet users

**Highlight this in your applications!** It's a unique advantage.

**Startup vs Big Company:**
- **Startups:** More ownership, faster growth, higher risk
- **Big companies:** Stable, better comp, narrower scope

Choose based on your career stage and risk tolerance.

**Design Maturity:**
- **High maturity:** CRED, Atlassian, Google, Adobe (design-first culture)
- **Growing maturity:** Razorpay, Swiggy, Freshworks (building design teams)
- **Low maturity:** Many early startups (you'll build design culture)

Consider where you want to be in the design org evolution.
